# C6 — Print-on-Demand

**One-liner:** your designs on t-shirts/mugs via a print service; you never touch stock, Telegram is the shop window.

## What it is
Print-on-demand (POD) = a service (e.g. Printful) prints and ships each order; we only supply the design and the store link. Customer orders in our Telegram storefront (channel + bot or direct link), Printful prints and ships, we keep the margin. Zero inventory, zero shipping work.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** buyers of the merch.
- **How much:** typical margin €8–15 per item after print+ship costs.
- **How often:** per sale; needs volume to matter.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** we own the design and the audience. Printful is a supplier, easily swapped.
- **Day-400:** designs + community = the moat. Nothing gets cut.

## Build steps
- [ ] Pick a niche with real demand (Dutch humor/culture, anime quiz merch, statiegeld jokes — your world has endless material)
- [ ] 5–10 designs (human-made or sourced — no AI-generated art, your rule)
- [ ] Printful account, connect a storefront (or just link each product in the channel)
- [ ] Telegram channel as the shop window + order link
- [ ] Post mockups, run a "first 10 orders" launch post

## Traffic engine
- The channel IS the audience; every post shows the product
- Buyers post photos → repost → more buyers (user-generated content)
- Cross-promo: quiz share card mentions the merch ("wear your streak")

## Hermes sustain role
- Trend monitoring (what's hot this week — I track, you decide)
- Listing copy, product descriptions, launch posts
- Order funnel tracking (which design sells, which flops)
- Renew the catalog monthly

## Launch speed
2–3 weeks to first sale (design + store setup + audience push).

## Risks
- Design taste is everything — a bad design sells zero. Start with 5, kill the losers.
- Thin margins mean volume matters; this is a slow burner, not a jackpot.

## First 3 moves
1. Pick the niche + first 5 design ideas.
2. Create designs (you draw/decide, I do copy and mockups).
3. Open the storefront and announce in the channel.
