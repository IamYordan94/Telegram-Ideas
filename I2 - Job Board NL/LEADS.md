## 2. Where the "need people NOW" posters live

These people are already advertising for workers, informally and free. They are the warmest leads.

- **Facebook groups** — search: `werk gezocht Amsterdam`, `horeca personeel gezocht`, `Amsterdam expats jobs`, `werk in Amsterdam`. Every day: "zoek vandaag 2 man voor verhuizing", "need kitchen help tonight".
- **Marktplaats** — Werk & vacatures category (`marktplaats.nl/l/werk-en-vacatures/...`): small employers post directly, no agency.
- **Telegram groups** — search inside Telegram: "Amsterdam werk", "horeca Amsterdam".
- **Your own building** — the facility + the companies around it. You stand in the middle of this market.
- **Every "zoek werk" post on your own feeds** — the flip side: workers posting "looking for work". Collect them into the channel; employers pay to be where the workers already are.

**Pitch rules (from the boss):** no hype, no cold email, no fake numbers. The pitch only works once /stats has a real number — "your ad in front of X workers at 8:00" is the whole sentence.

## 4. The CV point (framing fix, agreed with Veria)

The chat button is **first contact**, not the hire. Workers tap → message the employer → the employer screens their normal way (CV, call, trial day). For moving/horeca/cleaning, a trial day or 5-minute call often IS the screening. We never promise "no CV" — we promise "the boss is one tap away, your process after that is yours."
