# I2 — Job Board NL

**One-liner:** Telegram channel + bot where employers pay to post jobs; workers join free and get instant alerts.

## What it is
A job board for the NL labor market — logistics, agriculture/seasonal work, cleaning, horeca, construction. Employers (companies and temp agencies / uitzendbureaus) pay to post vacancies; workers join free and get alerts the moment a job goes up. The gap it fills: international workers don't use Indeed — they live in WhatsApp/Telegram groups, and agencies struggle to reach them fast. You know this market personally; that's the moat.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** employers / uitzendbureaus.
- **How much:** €15–25 per post, monthly packs (10 posts/€100), pinned/featured = extra, premium worker tier €2–5/month for instant alerts.
- **How often:** every vacancy; agencies post constantly once they see it works.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** we own the channel, the worker list, and the employer relationships. Telegram hosts it but the marketplace is ours.
- **Day-400:** agencies on monthly packs = recurring revenue. This is the strongest content-business on the list.

## Build steps
- [ ] Start with ONE sector + region (e.g. logistics, Amsterdam area)
- [ ] Channel + bot: post format, alert routing, paywall for employers (I build it)
- [ ] Seed: scrape public vacancies (Indeed/public boards) so the channel has daily jobs from day 1
- [ ] Grow workers: post in laborer groups, share cards, word of mouth at work
- [ ] When worker numbers are real, sell the first employer pack: "your vacancy in front of X workers today"
- [ ] Premium worker tier: instant alert before the daily digest

## Traffic engine
- Workers join for real jobs → tell coworkers (this market is word-of-mouth by nature)
- Employers pay once they see a real worker pool
- The daily job digest = the habit; instant alerts = the premium

## Hermes sustain role
- The entire back-end: posting bot, alert routing, paywall, moderation
- Daily vacancy scraping/drafting for the seed phase
- Chasing renewals (bot reminders to agencies)
- Stats: which jobs fill fastest (that's the sales argument)

## Launch speed
1–2 months to the first paying employer (grow workers first).

## Risks
- Two-sided marketplace: needs BOTH workers and employers. Seed jobs keep workers; real workers bring employers.
- Job quality: one fake/bad vacancy hurts trust — moderate everything.
- Legal: stick to real vacancies from real employers; no personal data beyond a Telegram username.
