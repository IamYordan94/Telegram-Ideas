# H3 — Support Bots for Businesses

**One-liner:** sell businesses an answering bot for their Telegram — setup fee + monthly retainer. Easiest B2B face-work.

## What it is
Businesses get flooded with the same questions (opening hours, prices, booking, directions). A support bot answers all of it instantly, 24/7, and hands the hard cases to a human. You sell the business a bot (one-time setup) plus a monthly retainer for hosting + updates. The deliverable is a product — one sale, then it runs — which makes this the easiest B2B option for you as the face.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** businesses with real customer traffic (restaurants, salons, gyms, service shops).
- **How much:** €100–400 setup + €25–75/month retainer.
- **How often:** recurring — 10 clients at €50/month = €500/month before new sales.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** we own the client + the bot infrastructure.
- **Day-400:** retainers compound; clients who leave are replaced. Real business.

## Build steps
- [ ] Build ONE reusable support-bot framework (FAQ answers + human handoff) — I do it
- [ ] Demo bot for a fictional restaurant
- [ ] One-page offer (plain language: "your customers get answers at 2am")
- [ ] Prospect: walk into 10 local businesses, show the demo on your phone
- [ ] Onboard: collect their FAQ, I configure, you deliver in days
- [ ] Monthly: send the value report ("your bot answered 342 questions this month")

## Traffic engine
- Walk-in demos (the demo IS the pitch)
- Referrals from every client
- The monthly report is a renewal argument written for you

## Hermes sustain role
- Build the framework + each client's configuration
- Hosting, uptime, fixes
- Add new Q&A when the business sends them
- The monthly value report (I generate, you forward)

## Launch speed
3–6 weeks to first paying client (1 demo + 10 visits).

## Risks
- Businesses that don't get Telegram questions won't renew — target ones that already have Telegram presence
- Face-time: you still must walk in. But after the sale, the bot does 95% of the work — lightest ongoing face-work of all H options.
