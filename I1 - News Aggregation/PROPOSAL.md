# I1 — News Aggregation

**One-liner:** a daily digest channel in one niche; sponsored posts pay for it. The machine writes it, you approve.

## What it is
A channel that posts the day's 5 most important things in one niche — every day, at the same time. Followers get a 2-minute read instead of scrolling; advertisers pay to reach that daily audience. Niche = higher ad value than general news. The daily gathering + drafting is automated; you approve in minutes.

**Niche examples (pick one):**
- **Amsterdam transport/OV** — delays, strikes, changes (commuters check daily)
- **Crypto NL** — the day's top 5, no hype
- **Work in NL** — labor-market news for international workers (ties to your world)

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** advertisers who want the niche audience (sponsored posts €20–50 each) + Telegram Ads revenue share (automatic 50% cut).
- **How much:** starts small, scales with subscriber count.
- **How often:** per sponsored post; Telegram Ads pays passively per view.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** we own the audience + the curation taste. Telegram hosts it.
- **Day-400:** the daily habit is the asset; advertisers come and go, the audience stays.

## Build steps
- [ ] Pick the niche
- [ ] Build the cron scraper: sources → top 5 items → draft post (I do it)
- [ ] You approve daily (5 min), bot publishes at a fixed time
- [ ] Grow: share cards, cross-posts into niche groups
- [ ] Sell sponsored slots once past ~500 real subscribers

## Traffic engine
- Fixed daily post time = habit (people open it with coffee)
- "Top 5" format is shareable by nature
- Real-time niche info spreads fast in the niche's own groups

## Hermes sustain role
- Daily scraping + drafting via cron (near-zero effort from you)
- Source monitoring (which sources are reliable, which die)
- Sponsored post copy when advertisers come
- Growth ideas + stats

## Launch speed
1 week to a working daily channel.

## Risks
- News burnout: the cron never misses, but YOU must approve daily or it stalls. (We can switch to auto-publish with an undo window later.)
- Advertisers only come with real numbers — first 500 subscribers is the grind.
