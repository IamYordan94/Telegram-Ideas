# D2 — Deal Channel

**One-liner:** a channel that posts daily deals with your affiliate link — the machine writes it, you approve.

## What it is
"Deals NL"-style channel: every day, 2–5 genuinely good deals (tech, home, fashion, subscriptions) with your affiliate link. Followers come for the savings, you get commission on every purchase. The daily scraping + drafting is automated; you only hit approve.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** merchants via affiliate programs.
- **How much:** per sale — deal channels convert well because people arrive ready to buy.
- **How often:** daily, steady — the volume comes from posting *every day*.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** merchants/networks. Same as D1 — keep 3+ programs so none can kill us.
- **Day-400:** the audience (people who want deals daily) is the asset; links are swappable.

## Build steps
- [ ] Pick scope: "Deals NL" (Dutch retailers) or a niche (tech deals, anime merch deals)
- [ ] Join Bol.com partner + Amazon DE + 1–2 others
- [ ] Cron scraper: pull daily deals each morning, draft posts (I build it)
- [ ] You approve in 5 minutes/day, posts go out
- [ ] Grow: share cards, cross-posts, "deal of the day" pinned

## Traffic engine
- Daily habit: people check the channel like a newspaper
- One "holy shit" deal gets shared and brings 50 followers
- Pinned "today's best deal" keeps the channel alive in people's chat list

## Hermes sustain role
- Daily scraping + drafting (cron, zero effort from you)
- Filter junk deals (only real discounts — trust is the product)
- Track which deals convert, adjust what we post
- Monthly report: best category, best day, what to post more

## Launch speed
1 week (program signups take a few days to approve).

## Risks
- Fake "deals" kill trust — we only post real price drops.
- Some retailers block affiliate links in some contexts — we stick to program rules.
- Needs consistent daily posting or followers drift away (the cron solves this).

## First 3 moves
1. Choose scope (all-NL vs niche).
2. Register affiliate accounts.
3. Build the daily scraper + approval flow.
