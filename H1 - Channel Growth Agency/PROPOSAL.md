# H1 — Channel Growth Agency

**One-liner:** grow brands' Telegram channels for a monthly retainer. I run the machine; you do a 30-minute call per month.

## What it is
Brands want a Telegram audience but don't know how: what to post, when, how to grow. You take a monthly retainer (€300–1,000) to run their channel: content calendar, posts, growth tactics, monthly report. The work is 80% machine (I draft content, automate posting, prepare reports); the 20% human is one client call per month — perfect for you as the face.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** brands with a Telegram presence but no in-house person (crypto projects, apps, local brands, e-commerce).
- **How much:** €300–1,000/month per client.
- **How often:** monthly retainer — 5 clients = €1,500–5,000/month.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** the client relationship — ours. The channel belongs to the client, but the *retainer* belongs to us.
- **Day-400:** retainers renew if numbers grow; reports prove the value.

## Build steps
- [ ] Define the offer: "Your channel, grown + run — €X/month" (content + posting + report)
- [ ] Build the toolbox: posting automation, content templates, growth playbook (I do it)
- [ ] Grow ONE case study first: a channel we run ourselves to a real number (proof before selling)
- [ ] Prospect: brands active on Telegram, DMs + referrals
- [ ] Deliver monthly: content calendar → posts → growth actions → report (I prepare, you present)

## Traffic engine
- The case study IS the sales pitch ("we grew ours from 0 to X")
- Referrals between brand owners (they all know each other)
- Public reports/threads showing results bring inbound leads

## Hermes sustain role
- Content drafts every week (you approve)
- Posting automation + scheduling
- Growth tactics (cross-promos, share hooks)
- The monthly report, formatted for you to present on the call

## Launch speed
1–2 months (case study first).

## Risks
- Growth isn't guaranteed — the first case study must be honest and real
- Client expectations: promise "we run it + report", never "we guarantee 10k followers"
- One bad client eats your month — keep retainers small until the system works

## First 3 moves
1. Pick the case-study channel topic.
2. I build the content + automation machine.
3. Grow the case study to a real number, then sell.
