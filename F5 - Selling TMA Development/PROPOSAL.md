# F5 — Selling TMA Development

**One-liner:** businesses want "their app inside Telegram" — you sell it, I build it. €1.5–5k each.

## What it is
Every business with a Telegram presence will eventually want a Mini App: a restaurant's menu + ordering, a gym's class booking, a shop's loyalty card, a service's booking form. This is E1 (freelance bot dev) but for the next wave — mini apps are newer, flashier, and priced higher. You sell the project, I build the app, we deliver.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** small/medium businesses.
- **How much:** €1,500–5,000 per mini app + €25–100/month maintenance.
- **How often:** per project; 1–2 projects/month is a serious income.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** the client relationship is ours. Telegram hosts the app but can't take the client.
- **Day-400:** maintenance retainers stack up = recurring income from past work.

## Build steps
- [ ] Build 2 demo mini apps (menu/order app, booking app) — I do it
- [ ] One-page pitch: "Your app, inside Telegram — live in 2 weeks" (plain Dutch/English)
- [ ] Prospect: businesses you can physically reach (restaurants, barbershops, gyms — walk in, show the demo on your phone)
- [ ] Deliver: I build, you demo, client pays
- [ ] Attach the maintenance retainer to every project

## Traffic engine
- Physical presence is the unfair advantage: most devs are remote and faceless; you can show the demo on your phone in person
- Referrals from every delivered project
- The demo apps are the marketing

## Hermes sustain role
- ~95% of the build (you review before the demo)
- Hosting, updates, fixes
- New-feature development per client request
- Quote drafting

## Launch speed
1–2 months to first client (2 demos first).

## Risks
- Same as E1: scope creep — fixed price, fixed scope, changes cost extra
- Sales is the bottleneck: you must walk into places. I can't.
- After-sales support is forever — the retainer covers it.

## First 3 moves
1. I build the 2 demo apps.
2. Define the offer + price.
3. You visit the first 5 businesses with the demo on your phone.
