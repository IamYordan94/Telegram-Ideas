# C1 — Digital Products

**One-liner:** make new digital products, sell them through a Telegram payment bot with instant delivery.

## What it is
Small digital things people buy for a few euros: templates, packs, mini-courses, planners. No stock, no shipping, made once and sold forever. Telegram is the storefront — the bot takes payment and delivers the file in 10 seconds.

**Example products (fresh, not existing ones):**
- Resume + cover letter pack for international workers in NL (English, simple, hiring-manager friendly)
- Budget spreadsheet (Excel/Sheets) for minimum-wage workers — hourly tracking, tips, statiegeld counting
- Printable weekly planner / shift-work planner
- "First day on the job in NL" phrasebook (English/Dutch workplace basics)

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** anyone, mostly via the channel / ads / word of mouth.
- **How much:** €5–25 per product, one-time.
- **How often:** passive — the bot sells while we sleep.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** we own the product and the customer list. Stripe processes payment only.
- **Day-400:** nobody can copy the *product + audience + trust* combination in one move.

## Build steps
- [ ] Pick ONE product (the resume pack is the strongest — job seekers in your world)
- [ ] Make the product (I generate the content/drafts, you approve)
- [ ] Payment bot with instant delivery (reuse the B5 order bot — same machine)
- [ ] Small channel as storefront + where to find buyers
- [ ] Post 2–3 free useful samples to pull people in

## Traffic engine
- Free samples posted in the channel → share → buyers
- Job-hunting groups (the product literally helps the people you already know)
- The buyer list grows with every sale → next product launches to warm buyers

## Hermes sustain role
- Generate + update product content (new resume styles, new language packs)
- Sales copy, product descriptions
- Delivery bot maintenance + support answers
- Watch which product sells, suggest the next one

## Launch speed
1–2 weeks for the first product.

## Risks
- Digital products get pirated — accept it; the buyer who pirates wasn't buying anyway.
- Quality bar: the product must actually be good. You have insider knowledge of this market, which most sellers don't — that's the moat.

## First 3 moves
1. Choose the first product.
2. I draft the full content.
3. Wire the order bot and sell the first 10.
