# D1 — Affiliate Links

**One-liner:** recommend products in a channel, get a cut of every sale through your link.

## What it is
Affiliate marketing = companies pay you a commission (usually 5–30%) when someone buys through your unique link. You run a Telegram channel, post recommendations/reviews with your link, and collect the cut. No product, no support, no shipping — just trust + links.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** the company (Amazon DE, Bol.com partner program, software companies, crypto exchanges, VPNs).
- **How much:** €0.50–€50+ per sale depending on the product.
- **How often:** every time someone buys via your link (cookies often give you the credit for days).

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** the merchant and the affiliate network. They can change terms or ban you.
- **Day-400:** your channel and audience survive even if one program dies — switch links, keep the audience. Don't depend on ONE program.

## Build steps
- [ ] Pick a niche you can talk about honestly (anime gear, work gear, crypto tools, budget tech)
- [ ] Join 2–3 affiliate programs (Bol.com partner, Amazon Associates, exchanges' referral programs)
- [ ] Start the channel, post 3–4 posts/week: "best X under €Y" with your link
- [ ] Always disclose it's an affiliate link (NL law requires it — one word: "ad" or "affiliate")
- [ ] Track which links convert, double down on winners

## Traffic engine
- Useful, honest posts get shared — that's the whole game
- Cross-post into related public groups (where allowed)
- One viral "best budget X" list can feed the channel for months

## Hermes sustain role
- Research products + programs, verify what pays what
- Draft the posts (you approve — your voice stays yours)
- Track clicks/conversions, kill dead links
- Find new programs when old ones drop rates

## Launch speed
Days — register, post, link.

## Risks
- Trust: pushing junk kills the channel permanently. Only recommend what you'd actually use.
- Some programs need minimum sales before payout (check the small print).
- No cold DM spam, no fake testimonials (your rules — and NL law backs them).

## First 3 moves
1. Choose the niche.
2. Register for 2 programs.
3. Write the first 3 posts together.
