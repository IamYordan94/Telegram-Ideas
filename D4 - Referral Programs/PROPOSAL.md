# D4 — Referral Programs

**One-liner:** post "sign up with my link, we both get something" — you get paid per signup.

## What it is
Companies pay you (€5–50) when someone signs up through your referral link: Revolut, N26, crypto exchanges, phone plans, energy providers. You post the link in a channel with an honest "what's in it for you, what's in it for me" explanation. Easiest money on this list to start — no product, no build.

## Money flow
- **Who pays:** the company running the referral program.
- **How much:** €5–50 per qualifying signup (often the new user gets a bonus too — that's the hook).
- **How often:** per signup; posts keep working for weeks.

## Pipe test
- **Who owns the pipe:** the company. They can end the program tomorrow.
- **Day-400:** zero moat — this is cash flow, not a business. Use it as side income while building something with a moat. Never depend on one program.

## Build steps
- [ ] List the best-paying NL-available programs (I research current offers)
- [ ] Start a channel or use an existing one — but keep it honest and relevant
- [ ] Post each offer as "here's the deal, here's the catch" (no hype — your rule)
- [ ] Track which links convert
- [ ] Refresh offers monthly (they change constantly)

## Traffic engine
- Honest posts get shared: "free €25 for both of us" is shareable if the offer is real
- Small, targeted groups beat shouting into big ones
- One good post can convert for months

## Hermes sustain role
- Monthly scan: which programs pay best right now, which died
- Draft the posts (you approve)
- Track signups/payouts per link
- Flag programs that stop paying

## Launch speed
Days. Sign up for 2–3 programs and post.

## Risks
- Offers expire or change terms — keep posts fresh or people call you out.
- Some programs require the referred person to actually *use* the product (deposit money, make a purchase) before you get paid. Read the fine print.
- Reputation: only post offers you'd take yourself.

2. You register for 2.
